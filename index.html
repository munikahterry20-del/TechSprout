THE WHY

The digital skills gap needs a practical answer.

TechSprout is designed around real constraints in high-density urban schools: limited hardware, theory-heavy ICT learning, and too few opportunities to develop communication and creative confidence.

01

Students deserve more than screen time and theory.

✓ Hardware deficits

Some schools can have 10–15 learners sharing one PC, limiting hands-on practice.

✓ Theory over practice

ICT assessment can emphasize exams instead of building, testing and creating.

✓ Soft-skill gap

Learners need stronger public speaking, pitching and digital communication skills.

THE TECHSPROUT SOLUTION

Lab-in-a-Box + station rotation + project portfolios.

Portable hardware hubs, offline-capable learning stations and project-based learning turn scarce resources into repeatable practical experiences.
